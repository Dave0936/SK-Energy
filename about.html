Meet SK Energy

We install solar PV, battery storage, EV charge points and air source heat pumps for residential and commercial clients.

Our mission is simple: reliable, efficient energy that lowers your costs and reduces your impact. Whether you want to generate your own power, store it for later, or heat your home more efficiently, we have the expertise to do it properly.

Our engineers and technicians are highly skilled and stay at the forefront of the industry. From first consultation to installation and ongoing support, one team looks after every stage.

Our story

From underfloor heating to whole-home renewables

We started out as an underfloor heating company, helping customers enjoy warm, comfortable homes. As the shift to renewable energy gathered pace, we saw our skills and values fit a bigger goal.

That grounding in efficient heating and tailored design is the foundation of everything we install today — and it's why we look at the whole property, not just one piece of kit.
